## 摘要
我们提出了首个（有界）值迭代算法，用于随机奇偶博弈的定量分析——这是具有ω-正则目标概率验证的基本模型。现有算法基于策略迭代，通过固定一方玩家反复计算另一方玩家的最优策略，导致计算成本高昂。而我们的算法直接作用于获胜概率的格论特征，利用奇偶目标下（几乎必然定性）获胜状态的结构性质，证明了所提算法的正确性与收敛性。

## Abstract
We present the first (bounded) value iteration algorithm for the quantitative analysis of stochastic parity games, a fundamental model for probabilistic verification with $ω$-regular objectives. Existing algorithms are based on strategy iteration, which repeatedly computes optimal strategies for one player while fixing the other, leading to high computational cost. Our algorithm instead operates directly on a lattice-theoretic characterization of winning probabilities, exploiting structural properties of (almost-sure qualitative) winning states under parity objectives. We prove correctness and convergence of the proposed algorithm.
