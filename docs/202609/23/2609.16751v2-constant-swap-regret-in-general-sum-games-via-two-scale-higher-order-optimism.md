## 摘要
我们给出了在完全信息反馈下针对有限多人一般和博弈的确定性与非耦合学习动力学，其在自博弈中实现常数个体交换遗憾，且与时间范围 $T$ 无关。当有 $n$ 名玩家且每人至多 $m$ 个动作时，每个玩家在每个有限时间范围上的个体交换遗憾为 $O(\sqrt n\,m\log m\log^{5/2}(nm))$。该动力学采用带乐观机制的经典 Blum-Mansour 框架。每位玩家预测偏离收益，利用这些预测更新一个行随机转移矩阵，并按其平稳分布进行行动。我们的新要素包括一个定制的行归一化映射和一个双尺度高阶预测器。通过一个通用的公共前缀切换包装器得到的对抗鲁棒变体，在自博弈界上保持至多相差一个通用常数，并在对抗设置下保证个体交换遗憾至多为 $7\sqrt{mT\log m}$。

## Abstract
We give deterministic and uncoupled learning dynamics for finite multiplayer general-sum games under full-information feedback that achieve constant individual swap regret in self-play, independent of the horizon $T$. With $n$ players and at most $m$ actions each, every player's individual swap regret is $O(\sqrt n\,m\log m\log^{5/2}(nm))$ at every finite horizon. The dynamics use the classical Blum-Mansour framework with optimism. Each player predicts the deviation gains, uses these predictions to update a row-stochastic transition matrix, and plays its stationary distribution. Our new ingredients include a tailored row normalization map and a two-scale higher-order predictor. An adversarially robust variant, obtained through a generic common-prefix switching wrapper, preserves the self-play bound up to a universal constant and guarantees individual swap regret at most $7\sqrt{mT\log m}$ in the adversarial setting.
